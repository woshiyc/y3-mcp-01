A top-down fantasy desert maze map, rendered in a detailed illustrated game-map style. The maze is composed of towering golden sand dunes forming winding corridor walls, their ridges casting dramatic shadows under a warm amber sky. At the very center of the maze lies a lush oasis — a vivid circular paradise with a shimmering turquoise pool surrounded by tall palm trees, exotic flowering shrubs, and vibrant emerald grass, glowing softly with magical bioluminescence. Scattered around the oasis are ancient stone ruins and crumbling sandstone pillars hinting at a lost civilization. The maze perimeter is bordered by massive crescent-shaped dunes rising like fortress walls. Hidden throughout the desert maze are subtle entrances to underground tunnels — revealed by dark stone archways half-buried in sand, wooden trapdoors camouflaged with sand texture, and faint glowing runes etched into the ground marking secret passages. The map includes a compass rose in one corner, illustrated in an antique parchment style. Warm golden and ochre color palette with deep purple shadows. High detail, isometric-like perspective, fantasy RPG cartography art style, aged parchment texture overlay, cinematic lighting.
